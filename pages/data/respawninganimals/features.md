* 🔄 **Bedrock-Style Spawning:** Animals now spawn and disappear around players like they do in Bedrock Edition, rather than staying in the world forever.
* 📏 **Distance-Based Removal:** Animals beyond a set distance from the nearest player may be removed over time, with a farther hard cutoff that removes them instantly.
* 🌱 **Repopulation Around Players:** When too few animals remain near a player, new ones spawn nearby to fill the area back in.
* 🔒 **Player Animals Stay Permanent:** Feeding, taming, leashing, riding, or naming an animal protects it, so pets and livestock are never removed by distance.
* ⚙️ **Per-World Game Rules:** Most behavior is controlled by world-specific game rules, which start enabled in new worlds and can be changed anytime with `/gamerule`.
* 🏷️ **Entity Tag Exclusions:** Any animal type added to the `respawninganimals:persistent_animals` entity tag never gets removed, giving mods and datapacks fine-grained control.
