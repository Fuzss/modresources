* 🧟 **Mutant Zombie:** A hulking brute with powerful smash attacks that drops the Hulk Hammer.
* 🏹 **Mutant Skeleton:** A deadly marksman whose bones can be crafted into Mutant Skeleton Armor.
* 💥 **Mutant Creeper:** Wreaks havoc with chain explosions and drops a Creeper Minion Egg.
* 🌀 **Mutant Enderman:** A teleporting terror whose severed hand grants the Endersoul Hand and its powers.
* ☃️ **Mutant Snow Golem:** A powerful ally that hurls massive chunks of ice at enemies.
* 🧨 **Creeper Minions:** Explosive allies hatched from a Creeper Minion Egg and tracked with the Creeper Minion Tracker.
* 🕷️ **Spider Pig:** A fast, unpredictable creature created by using Chemical X on a pig that has eaten a fermented spider eye.
* 🧪 **Chemical X:** A mysterious mutagen that transforms mobs into powerful mutants or strange new creatures.
